02 / Design directions

How can we help users build?

AI can be amazing and still feel inaccessible if you’re not technical. My hypothesis: turn the technical mental load into a clear visual roadmap, so first-time builders can follow the work and learn as they go. I brainstormed three ways to do that.

Independent concept mockups · AI-generated · sample data. No live Claude run.

03 / Chosen direction · A

A roadmap. The work beside it.

I chose A because the roadmap stays separate from the work. Users can see where their project stands without losing the conversation or preview.

I designed ‘Build your first tool’ as the center of the workspace, where Claude works, shares live updates and talks with the user. The roadmap stays on the left; the tool takes shape on the right. My aim is to make the plan easier to follow, invite participation, make progress visible and help first-time builders get building.

What to test next

Can first-time builders identify their current step, understand what is next, and resume a small project unaided?
