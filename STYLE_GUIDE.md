# ijidakinro.com style guide (for AI agents and collaborators)

Read this before you touch any page. Goal: every page on this site looks, sounds and behaves like the homepage (`index.html`), and every claim on it is true.

Owner: Adé Ijidakinro ("Ube"). Pronouns are not stated; use they/them in anything written about them. The site is written in first person as Adé.

## 0. The ten rules
1. **Truth first.** Every number and claim traces to a source (section 7). Never invent a metric, title, date, client or quote. If a fact is missing, write `[[CONFIRM: question]]` and ask Ube.
2. **Plain words.** Say what Adé did, decided, measured, and what the limits were. No hype ("passionate", "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "10x").
3. **Black, white and one idea per section.** No accent colors. Personality comes from the serif italic, the octopus, and honest stories.
4. **Use the tokens and components that exist** (sections 3 and 4). Do not add colors, fonts or one-off styles.
5. **Light and dark both work.** Never hard-code a color; use `var(--...)`.
6. **375px phone width must work** with no horizontal scroll.
7. **Motion earns its place** and switches off for `prefers-reduced-motion` (section 5).
8. **Edit the source, not the output.** `index.html` is generated (section 1).
9. **Ask Ube before publishing** (pushing to `main`) unless they said to publish.
10. **Run the checks** in section 9 before you say anything is done.

## 2. Brand and voice
- **Name:** Adé Ijidakinro. Public role line: *AI Enablement · Program Management · Learning & Workflow Design.* Job title on every page and résumé: **Program Manager, AI & Learning** (Ehoro Village). Do not invent other titles.
- **Hero line:** "I help teams adopt AI and improve how work gets done." The last words of a headline go in the serif: `<span class="serif">work gets done.</span>`
- **Mark:** a minimal octopus (eight arms, round head). Generated by `octo.py`; inline it as SVG so it follows `currentColor` and dark mode. Never recolor it, never add a gradient. The line that goes with it: "An octopus keeps about two-thirds of its neurons in its arms. Each arm works on its own; the head sets the intent."
- **Tone:** direct, warm, a little dry. First person. Short sentences. Name responsibilities, decisions, outcomes and limits. About 60% recruiter-friendly, 40% personality (the octopus, the sushi quote, the carrot allergy, honest failure stories).
- **Names:** use a plain label with the fun name in parentheses: "Decision-maker (the Overlord)", "Planner (the Underlord)". The homepage lessons and map follow this.
- **Numbers:** write them the way the page does: `3 weeks → 4–7 days`, `18% → 32%`, `11 regional project managers`. Use an en dash for ranges and `→` for before and after. Credit honestly: "which I helped increase", "in a two-person pilot", "functional prototype".
- **Do not write:** em-dash-heavy slogans, "journey", "leverage", "synergy", fake testimonials, or any claim about users, revenue or adoption that is not in section 7's sources.

## 3. Design tokens (defined in `site.css` `:root`)
| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#ffffff` | `#0a0a0a` | page |
| `--bg-2` | `#f6f6f5` | `#121212` | tinted blocks (stat cards, open rows, asides) |
| `--card` | `#ffffff` | `#101010` | cards |
| `--ink` | `#0b0b0b` | `#f2f2f0` | text, primary buttons, active states |
| `--ink-2` | `#3a3a3a` | `#c9c9c6` | body copy |
| `--muted` | `#737373` | `#8d8d89` | captions, labels |
| `--line` / `--line-2` | `#e7e7e5` / `#d6d6d3` | `#222220` / `#33332f` | hairlines / stronger borders |
| `--live` | `#16a34a` | same | the green status dot only |

- **Fonts (Google Fonts):** Inter 400/500/600/700 (UI and body), Instrument Serif regular + italic (headline accent only), JetBrains Mono 400/500 (labels, tags, numbers in captions).
- **Type scale:** body 16px/1.6; hero h1 `clamp(40px, 5.4vw, 74px)` weight 600, tracking -.045em; h2 `clamp(32px, 4.2vw, 52px)` weight 600, tracking -.04em; h3 19px weight 600; lede `clamp(17px, 1.6vw, 19px)`; labels 11.5px mono, uppercase, .14em tracking.
- **Layout:** `.wrap` max-width 1120px, 24px side gutters; sections have 104px top padding (80px on phones); cards use radius 16–22px, 1px `--line` borders, no heavy shadows (`--shadow` only on floating pills).
- **Buttons:** pill, 42px tall (`.small` 36px). `.btn` is solid ink; `.btn.ghost` is outlined. At most one solid button per group.
- **Breakpoints:** 1000px and 820px. At 820px the nav collapses into the menu button and multi-column grids go to one column.
- **Easing:** `var(--ease)` = `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)`.

Rules: one `h1` per page. Use `.reveal` on blocks that should fade in as they scroll. Put long text in `.prose` (max 740px, aligned to the page's left edge). Images: `loading="lazy"`, real `alt` text, width/height when known, keep under ~300 KB.

## 5. Motion and interaction (all in `site.js`)
- **Entrance:** hero headline words slide up (`.w > span`, 45ms stagger); `.fade` elements ease in; `.reveal` blocks rise 18px over .8s when they enter the viewport (IntersectionObserver).
- **Count-up:** `data-count="N"` on a number counts up once in 1.4s. Only for plain integers.
- **Magnetic buttons:** `data-magnetic` on `.btn` (fine pointers only).
- **Theme toggle:** sets `data-theme` on `<html>` and saves `localStorage.theme`; the inline script in `<head>` applies it before paint.
- **Progress line** at the top, **sticky header** with a hairline after scrolling, **active section** highlighting in the nav.
- **Octopus:** pupils follow the cursor, the body leans a few degrees, it waves on click and once after load, arms sway on a loop.
- **Tabs and switches:** a pill slides under the active option; arrow keys move between tabs.
- **System map:** curved wires with moving dots; clicking a node explains it; it auto-cycles until the visitor clicks.
- **Must always hold:** `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off animation (the CSS has the global override); nothing autoplays sound or video; no scroll-jacking; no motion longer than ~1.4s except the ambient octopus sway.
- **New interactions:** add them to `site.js` behind an element-exists check so inner pages without that element do not break.

## 6. Accessibility and responsive
- Skip link, one `h1`, ordered headings, landmarks (`header`, `main`, `footer`, `nav` with `aria-label`).
- Keyboard: every control reachable and visibly focused (the focus ring comes from `:focus-visible`); tabs support arrow keys; accordions are real buttons with `aria-expanded`.
- Contrast: body text uses `--ink-2` on `--bg` in both themes; keep `--muted` for non-essential captions only.
- Phones: test at 375×812. No horizontal scrolling (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth` must equal `innerWidth`). Long chips and buttons must wrap.
- Links: external links `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`. PDFs state their type in the link text.

## 8. Making or converting pages
**New page**
```
python _build/new_page.py my-page --title "Plain words and serif words" --label "Case study" --description "One sentence."
```
Fill every `{{...}}` (`python _build/new_page.py --check my-page.html` lists what is left), keep the section order (hero, facts strip, numbers, Problem / What I did / The result / Limits), and link back to `index.html#work`, never to a legacy page.

**Convert a legacy case study** (`stress-tester.html`, `analyzer.html`, `build-with-ai.html`, `braid.html`)
1. Generate a new page with `new_page.py` under a temporary slug.
2. Move the facts across (not the old CSS, fonts, pink accent or layout). Keep downloadable files in `assets/`.
3. Replace the old `.back-nav` and footer link block with the template's crumb and footer.
4. Check every claim against section 7 (the old pages predate the cleanup), rename to the final file name, and verify the homepage links still resolve.
Legacy pages use Fraunces, Outfit and a pink accent (`#d4658a`). Do not copy those.

**Other sites for Ube.** ehorovillage.com shares this design DNA (Inter, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono, black and white, dark mode) but is a company site: no founder, no bio, no prices, no games, visible contact text "Email us". Do not mix the two sites' content rules.

## 9. Before you say it is done

2. Open the page in a browser at 1280px and 375px, in light and dark. Scroll the whole page: no overlap, no overflow, no layout jump.
3. Console has no errors from `site.css`/`site.js`.
4. Every internal link and `#anchor` resolves. (Quick check: collect `href`/`src` values from each page and request them from a local `python -m http.server`.)
5. Search the built HTML for anything in section 7's "never publish" list.
6. Every number matches its source; update the `SOURCES` comment.
7. Keyboard test: Tab through the header, tabs, accordions and buttons.
8. Do not publish until Ube says so (or already told you to).
